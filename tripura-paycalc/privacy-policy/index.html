Privacy Policy

The policy below matches the privacy information provided inside Tripura PayCalc.

Published 15 October 2026

Tripura PayCalc is an independent utility by Sanat Utility Studio. Contact: sanatutilitystudio@gmail.com.

Your calculator information

Salary, basic pay, allowances, deductions and arrears you enter are calculated on your device in working app memory. There are no accounts, backend salary storage or salary uploads, saved salary histories or profiles. Android backup is disabled. Calculations work offline. Salary figures are not sent to Google AdMob for advertising targeting.

Google advertising is separate

This free app uses Google AdMob Mobile Ads SDK. Google may process IP addresses, advertising identifiers, device and app information, ad interactions and approximate location inferred from network information for ad delivery, performance measurement and fraud prevention. This third-party processing is governed by Google’s privacy policy. The SDK may store limited consent and ad-frequency metadata independently of salary information. Google’s retention and data handling are governed by its own terms and policies.

Consent and privacy choices

Google UMP consent forms are used where applicable. Ad Privacy Choices explains whether privacy options are required for your session; a form is offered only when required. Google My Ad Center also provides controls for Google advertising. These choices concern advertising, not the calculator’s local salary data.

Support and your rights

If you email support, your email and information you choose to provide are used to respond. Avoid sending unnecessary salary or personal information. Contact sanatutilitystudio@gmail.com for privacy questions and requests concerning your rights. For Google advertising data, use Google’s privacy controls and request channels.

Adults and changes

The app is intended for adults. Changes to this policy will be reflected in the updated policy and publication date. Read the latest published policy for any changes.
