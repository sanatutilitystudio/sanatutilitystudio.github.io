Sanat Utility Studio

Website privacy & analytics

Effective 17 October 2026. This policy applies to sanatutilitystudio.github.io, not to separate app-specific privacy policies.

What the website does

This public website describes Sanat Utility Studio projects and publishes journal articles. Viewing its pages does not require an account. Standard website hosting may process basic access and security information as described in GitHub's privacy statement.

Optional Google Analytics

With your permission, the website uses Google Analytics 4 to understand traffic, page views, approximate geographic distribution, browser/device information and interactions with links to app downloads. Google Analytics can use cookies or similar identifiers and may process information in other countries. See Google's Privacy Policy.

We load no Google Analytics tag until you choose Allow analytics. Declining leaves GA4 disabled. We do not enable Google advertising personalization or use Google Analytics to collect medical or patient records through this website.

App-link clicks

If you opt in, clicks on Google Play app listings and GitHub release links may be recorded as events. We send the destination domain/path and the source page, not the query string or personally entered content. These events help us understand which software links are useful.

Your choices

Your choice is saved locally in your browser so the website remembers it on later visits. You can change it at any time; withdrawing consent reloads the page to stop loaded analytics. Blocking scripts or storage may prevent preferences from being remembered.

Other services and contact

Following external links to Google Play, GitHub and other websites subjects you to those services' own policies. We do not sell website visitors' personal information. For questions about this website, contact sanatutilitystudio@gmail.com.
