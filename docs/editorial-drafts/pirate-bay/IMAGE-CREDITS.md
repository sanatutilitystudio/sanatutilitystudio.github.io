## Changes to originals

The six Wikimedia images were downloaded directly using Commons file redirects and resized in the local approved project with high-quality bicubic resampling. All six were exported as quality-85 JPEGs at 850 to 1200 px width (poster 750 px). They are **format- and size-modified copies**, not byte-identical originals. Photo creators, direct source URLs and CC BY terms remain linked in figure captions; this document explicitly records the changes. Original downloads remain in the PC's `docs/editorial-assets/pirate-bay/originals/` research folder.
