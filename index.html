About

Independent by design.

Sanat Utility Studio is an independent software studio focused on practical digital tools. Projects range from health and productivity apps to audio, image and media utilities.

The goal is simple: build software with a clear purpose, avoid unnecessary complexity, and keep improving through real-world use.
